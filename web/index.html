Device Controls

Two-button navigation: UP (BOOT) and DOWN (PWR)

▲ Click
Short click BOOT / UP: Previous page (Reading) / Move cursor up (Menu & Library)
▼ Click
Short click PWR / DOWN: Next page (Reading) / Move cursor down (Menu & Library)
2x ▲
Double click BOOT / UP: Toggle bookmark (Reading) / Select item (Menu & Library)
2x ▼
Double click PWR / DOWN: Open bookmarks (Reading) / Select item (Menu & Library)
⏵ Hold
Long press (>1.2s) UP or DOWN: Open reader menu (Reading) / Back or exit (Menu, Library & Sync)
▲▼ Both
Hold together (2.0s) UP + DOWN: Force reboot device
⏻ Wake
Short click UP or DOWN: Wake device from deep sleep or start on boot
